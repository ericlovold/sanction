03The CLARITY moment

Scheduled news event · Senate cloture vote ~Sept 15

Prepare entirely in advance. Draft "What CLARITY §604 means for agent infrastructure": walk the non-controlling test in plain English, then show Sanction's architecture satisfying each prong in code — veto-only co-sign, keys upstream, funds upstream. Write two endings: passes ("the safe harbor agent infra was waiting for") and fails/stalls ("the test is still the right design constraint — here's why we built to it anyway").

Within hours of the vote: publish the matching version, founder thread, email the list, one-line pitch to crypto-policy desks (The Block, Blockworks). Standing: almost everyone commenting is a lawyer or an exchange — Sanction is a shipped product whose architecture is the §604 test implemented. Analysis, not legal advice; say so.

05Pro early-access funnel

  • Capture: every post, /govern page, and listing links to the pricing section — Lead model with source tags (hnlocalllamalobstersmcp-registryx402-ecoclarity-post), funnel-beacon on the page.
  • Honest labeling: "Early access — $20/mo, metered in decisions, billing hasn't started." Collect intent, not cards. No checkout, fake or real, until metering ships. (Live on /platform now ✓)
  • Activation drip (3 emails / ~10 days): ① "Your first decision in 5 minutes" — setup.sh, one /authorize call, the console screenshot. ② "Watch a week before you enforce" — observe mode, would-be denials with dollars attached. ③ "What $20 buys — and how we keep ourselves honest" — the fee meter, the operator-set fee cap enforced by our own engine, per-decision receipts, the grace-mode promise.
  • Conversion moment: when metered billing ships — 30 days' notice, founding price honored, and the decision meter already shows each wallet its exact would-be bill.
