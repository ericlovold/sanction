## GTM readiness and next steps

The website's current lead is **Human oversight. Autonomous agents.** The full
use-case experience, connection guide, terms, privacy and support are live.
Positioning should show a person handing work to an agent, the exact boundary
that requires review, and the evidence of what happened afterward. Cross-provider
collaboration belongs in that story without promising universal interception.

Activation remains on hold until the agreed connector scope is complete.
Preparation can continue: a sanitized approval demonstration, provider-specific
setup instructions, operator pain-point stories, and accurate listing links.
Avoid launching with an “available everywhere” claim while providers remain in
review or require custom connections.

Before activation, define completion per surface as: install/authentication,
correct agent identity, approval/pause, exact redemption, denial/replay/tamper,
expiry and disconnection behavior; then record the applicable distribution state.
Backend tests and a published listing cannot replace host evidence.

Measure the eventual funnel from connection → first requested approval → human
decision → exact redemption → observed governed action → repeat use. Current
`lib/funnel.ts` includes acquisition, gateway and Slack events but does not define
that entire cross-provider funnel. Instrument the missing stages as a separate
slice before evaluating activation. Historical visitor/event totals are not
account counts; no fresh account, adoption or revenue count was queried here.
